Art & Architecture

Tunisia is full of artistic details from colorful ceramic tiles to beautifully decorated doors.

Traditional architecture often features courtyards, arches, geometric patterns, and intricate tilework.

Look closely as you explore — some of the most beautiful details are hidden in everyday streets and buildings.

Tunisian Traditions & Hospitality

Hospitality plays an important role in Tunisian culture.

Sharing food, enjoying conversations, and welcoming guests are meaningful parts of everyday life.

Traditional crafts, celebrations, music, and family gatherings also help keep Tunisia’s cultural heritage alive.

A visit to Tunisia is an opportunity to experience these traditions and learn more about local life.

History

Tunisia's history has been shaped by many civilizations, including Amazigh communities, Phoenicians, Romans, and Arab dynasties.

Their influences can still be seen in the country's architecture, historical landmarks, art, and traditions.

Explore the ruins of Carthage, visit the impressive Roman amphitheatre of El Jem, or wander through the historic medinas.
